Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Study Track: Giant Stopwatch ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

The App is developed and published by Alok Apps. For privacy-related questions, you can contact us at alokapps.dev@gmail.com.

1. Information Stored by the App

Study Track: Giant Stopwatch allows you to record and manage your study sessions, including information such as selected dates, subject names, study durations, and related study history.

This study information is stored locally on your device using the App's local storage. We do not operate a personal account system for the App, and the App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal profile information to use its core study-tracking features.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements, including banner and full-screen advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes. Depending on Google's services and applicable settings, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers
  • Information about interactions with the App and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be collected and shared by Google in accordance with Google's applicable policies and terms. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

For more information about how Google handles information from apps that use its advertising services, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored locally by the App is used to provide features such as study-session tracking, study history, statistics, and session recovery.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads SDK may be used for purposes including delivering and measuring advertisements, advertising-related analytics, fraud prevention, and improving the reliability of Google's advertising services.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your locally stored study records or personal information.

Advertising-related information may be collected and shared by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in Google's applicable privacy policies and documentation.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Study records created within the App are stored locally on your device. You can manage or delete study records using the App's available history-management features.

Because these study records are stored locally rather than in an online account operated by us, we do not maintain a server-side copy of your study history.

Advertising-related information handled by Google is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices and applicable controls.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep information handled by the App appropriately protected. Study records are stored locally on your device.

Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encryption in transit, as described in Google's documentation.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or registration system.

Advertising services used by the App may have their own policies, restrictions, and age-related requirements. Google's policies and applicable settings govern the information processed by Google advertising services.

The App uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies. The principal third-party service used for advertising is:
